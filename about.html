About Us

Family-owned, community-focused, and committed to serving Elizabethtown the best Italian food.

Your Neighborhood Italian Restaurant

Welcome to My Place Italian Restaurant - Elizabethtown's go-to spot for delicious pizza, fresh pasta, mouthwatering hoagies, and so much more. We're not just another pizza place; we're your neighbors, and we treat every customer like family.

Our recipes have been perfected over the years, combining traditional Italian flavors with the hearty portions Pennsylvania is known for. Every dish is made fresh to order, using quality ingredients that you can taste in every bite.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick lunch, ordering dinner for the whole family, or catering your next event, we're here to make sure you leave satisfied - and knowing exactly what you paid.
